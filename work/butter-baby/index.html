Bakery / A Speckle project

Butter Baby

A generous helping of everyday joy.

Identity · Packaging · Print
Behind the idea

A world of
its own.

A warm, tactile identity for the small pleasure of something freshly baked. Butter-yellow packaging, soft handwritten details and a little cloche illustration make every box feel like a gift.

Story in progress — the following brief, challenge and approach are draft copy, awaiting the studio’s final project notes.

The brief

Create a bakery world that feels personal, generous and easy to recognise, from the first glimpse of a shopfront to the last cookie in the box.

The challenge

Balance an expressive name and playful details with the quiet simplicity that lets the food itself look delicious.

The solution

A flowing wordmark, a butter-dish motif and a warm yellow-and-cream palette carry across boxes, translucent wraps, cards and signage.

How the idea takes shape

From a spark
to a whole world.

Find the warmth

Start with the familiar textures of a bakery: crinkled foil, warm paper and the soft shapes of something made by hand.

Make a small signature

The cloche illustration becomes a repeatable brand detail, paired with loose lettering and a compact monogram.

Wrap up the feeling

Use the same thoughtful touches across tissue, takeaway bags and gift boxes, with plenty of space for the treats to shine.
