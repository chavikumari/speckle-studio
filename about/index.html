The people behind Speckle

Aliza & Chavi.
Two minds. One little studio.

A little note from the co-founder

Speckle started with a simple thought: good design begins with understanding.

I've always been curious about the stories behind things — the people, ideas and little details that give a brand its character. I like getting to know what's behind an idea before jumping into how it should look. I think that's where the interesting part of design begins.

That's what I wanted Speckle to be about. A space to explore ideas, ask questions, experiment a little and create work that feels thoughtful, individual and full of character.

Today, I'm building Speckle alongside Chavi, the other half of Speckle Studio. It's exciting to have another creative mind in the mix. We bring our own perspectives to the table, bounce ideas around, explore different possibilities and make things together.

Brand identity is at the heart of what we do, but we're just as happy exploring typography, editorial, print and all the little details that bring a brand to life.

We're still growing, learning and figuring out all the things Speckle can be. And honestly, that's one of the nicest parts of it. We're looking forward to the ideas we'll chase, the people we'll meet and the things we'll make along the way.

Thanks for stopping by. We're glad you're here.

Aliza

Co-Founder, Speckle Studio

A little note from the co-founder

Hi, I'm Chavi! The other half of Speckle Studio.

I've always loved the idea of making something out of nothing. A little sketch, a random thought, or even a colour combination can turn into something completely unexpected. And I think that's what I love most about design — there are so many possibilities, and there's always something new to explore.

I have a soft spot for playful branding, thoughtful packaging, quirky illustrations, interesting typography, and all the tiny details that make a brand feel like it has a personality of its own. I love when design doesn't just look good, but makes you feel something, even if it's just a little smile.

Speckle came together quite naturally. Aliza and I met while studying design, and somewhere along the way, we discovered how much we admired each other's work. What started as conversations about design slowly turned into sharing ideas, imagining possibilities, and eventually thinking, why don't we create something of our own?

And that's how Speckle began.

What makes it even more special is that we don't always think alike. We have our own styles, perspectives and ways of approaching things, and I love how that brings something different to the table. Sometimes one little idea turns into ten more, and that's usually when things get interesting.

For me, Speckle is a space where we can experiment, try things we haven't tried before, make a few happy accidents, and create work that feels personal, imaginative and full of character.

I'm especially drawn to building brand identities that go beyond just a logo. I love imagining how a brand would look on a box, a coffee cup, a storefront, or even the smallest little sticker. It's those details that make the whole thing come alive for me.

We're just getting started, and there's so much we want to create, learn and discover along the way. I'm excited for all the ideas that haven't happened yet, the people we'll get to work with, and all the little things that will make Speckle, well, Speckle.

Here's to creating things that are a little different, a little unexpected, and a whole lot of fun.

Chavi

Co-Founder, Speckle Studio
