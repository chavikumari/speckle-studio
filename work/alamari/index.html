Alamāri

A wardrobe of timeless treasures.

Identity · Illustration · Print
Behind the idea

A world of
its own.

An identity built around the wardrobe as a keeper of memories. Graceful lettering, illustrated craft details and rich textile colours create a world that feels intimate, considered and full of stories.

Story in progress — the following brief, challenge and approach are draft copy, awaiting the studio’s final project notes.

The brief

Develop a visual world for a textile brand that celebrates the emotional connection between what we wear and what we keep.

The challenge

Express a sense of heritage with restraint, allowing the textures, colours and craft of the textiles to remain central.

The solution

A distinctive wordmark and wardrobe illustration anchor the identity. Botanical, animal and embroidery motifs extend it across cards, tags and a simple cloth tote.

How the idea takes shape

From a spark
to a whole world.

Begin with a memory

The wardrobe becomes the central metaphor: a place for favourite pieces, familiar textures and stories carried forward.

Draw the details

A thread-holding tiger, embroidery hoop and draped chair add character to a flexible illustration family.

Let the material lead

Restrained layouts on cream paper and cloth make room for colour and textile texture to tell the story.
