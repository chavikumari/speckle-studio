Tea & ritual / A Speckle project

The Real Chai Company

Where every cup is a journey.

Identity · Packaging · Illustration
Behind the idea

A world of
its own.

A richly illustrated world for the ritual of chai. Botanical patterns, expressive lettering and warm colours connect the packaging with the everyday pleasure of slowing down over a cup.

Story in progress — the following brief, challenge and approach are draft copy, awaiting the studio’s final project notes.

The brief

Create a cohesive identity and packaging world that brings the warmth, character and social ritual of chai into every touchpoint.

The challenge

Balance intricate illustration with clear information. Carry the same personality from a cylindrical tea tin to a menu, a brewing card and a storefront.

The solution

Cream, deep red and leafy greens frame an expressive bilingual wordmark. Floral and animal motifs wrap the tins, while ornamental borders and small line illustrations unify the printed pieces.

How the idea takes shape

From a spark
to a whole world.

Start with the ritual

The idea centres on warmth and connection, using familiar cups, kettles and shared moments as visual cues.

Create a living pattern

Flowers, leaves, birds and animals form a detailed pattern that gives the packaging a distinctive presence.

Follow the whole journey

Menus, brewing cards, loyalty pieces and environmental graphics extend the identity beyond the product.
