Coffee & culture / A Speckle project

Main Character Coffee

Coffee with a little main-character energy.

Identity · Packaging · Brand experience
Behind the idea

A world of
its own.

A coffee world with a playful point of view. Cherry red, powder blue and soft pink connect expressive cups, takeaway carriers and a mobile coffee bar, turning an everyday stop into a small occasion.

Story in progress — the following brief, challenge and approach are draft copy, awaiting the studio’s final project notes.

The brief

Imagine a recognisable coffee identity that can live on a cup, travel in a hand and take over a whole coffee trailer.

The challenge

Bring an eclectic set of references together without losing clarity. Make the experience feel expressive at every scale, from a tiny loyalty stamp to a large sign.

The solution

Stripes, heart shapes and a confident wordmark build a flexible graphic language. Photography and playful props give the whole system an unmistakable personality.

How the idea takes shape

From a spark
to a whole world.

Set the scene

Colour leads the mood: cool blue and candy pink meet bold red for a lively, instantly recognisable combination.

Build the everyday pieces

Cups, carriers, loyalty cards and aprons turn the graphic language into objects people can use and keep.

Take it into the world

A coffee trailer, tactile signage and styled imagery make the brand feel like a place to step into.
